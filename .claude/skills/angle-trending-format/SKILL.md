# Angle: trending-format

Borrow the shape that works this week; the content stays ours.

## Where the input comes from
- **Substack**: `gold.substack_trending` (dimension `substack-trending`): the recent posts of finance publications we follow (public archive JSON through each publication's own domain, never substack.com from GitHub Actions), ranked by reactions + 2 x restacks + comments over the last 30 days. The writer gets the top post's title, subtitle and engagement.
- **X**: no free source exists, and the repo never scrapes X. X formats come from the curated list below, kept by hand from what big finance accounts do now.
- Sandbox: slot 17:00, the format applied to the day's third Idea.

## Curated X formats (Nov 2026)
1. **BREAKING one-liner + chart**: « BREAKING: » + headline + cashtag + chart (Kobeissi Letter pattern: https://superx.so/creators/KobeissiLetter).
2. **One number, one chart**: a single figure as the hook, the chart as the proof.
3. **X vs Y comparison**: two figures side by side (« Revenue +20%. Stock -56%. »).
4. **Earnings in one line**: the quarter's key figure vs expectations.
5. **Single long post over a thread**: one long post beat a thread on the same topic (Hootsuite test: https://blog.hootsuite.com/experiment-x-threads-vs-longform-posts).
The writer picks the one that fits the Idea best.

## How to write it
- Keep the trending post's SHAPE: title pattern, length, structure, hook type. The hook still states OUR claim with its number; never a teaser or question that holds back the fact.
- Fill it with OUR Idea, its figures only. The text's lead number is the one the chart and note show, over the same period (a quarterly sales figure gets the quarterly chart, a gross margin text the gross margin chart), and the text makes ONE claim.

## Never
- Copy a sentence or a figure of the trending post.
- Name the publication we borrowed the shape from.
