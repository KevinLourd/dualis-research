# Angle: competitor

Learn from what the best independent research writers publish: borrow the pattern, never the lines (the site already bans Aurelion's lines in `site/src/lib/copy.test.ts`).

## Where the input comes from
- `silver.content_posts` of our reference publications (`REFERENCE_PUBLICATIONS` in `content-posts`: Citrini Research, Aurelion Research, Archetype Capital, DeepValue Capital, Liger Cub, Multibagger Ideas), collected daily from their public feeds, each with a 2,000-character excerpt.
- Sandbox: slot 20:00; the input = one of their posts of the last 60 days (rotates by day), applied to the day's fourth Idea.

## How to write it
- Name the PATTERN for yourself first (a closed-call scoreboard, a « primer » that explains an industry, a teaser with 3 figures, a contrarian question, a field note).
- Rebuild that pattern with our Idea and its figures.

## Never
- Copy a sentence, a title or a figure from their post.
- Name or tag the competitor.
- Claim their call or their numbers as ours.
