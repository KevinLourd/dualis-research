# Content matrix (Kevin 2026-11-11)

A post = **channel x tone x format x angle**. Each axis has its skill; the writer reads them as they are written here.

| Axis | Values | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Channel | X, Substack | the channel x tone skill |
| Tone | urgent, balanced, neutral | `x-urgent`, `x-balanced`, `x-neutral`, `substack-urgent`, `substack-balanced`, `substack-neutral` |
| Format | X post, X Article (X); Note, Article (Substack) | a section of the channel x tone skill |
| Angle | signal only (Kevin 2026-11-13: « on va se focus sur nos signaux ») | `angle-signal` |

One post = the channel x tone skill (its format section) + the angle skill of that post. Balanced = today's house style (skills `x-post`, `daily-content`, `research-note`, `breaking-chart`); the urgent and neutral skills move ONLY the tone and keep every other rule.

## Rules every post keeps, whatever the tone
- A signal post (BUY or SELL) follows the 5-step skeleton of `angle-signal` (type, size, trigger + date, rationale per driver with dates, conclusion), one step per line; it overrides each tone's length and « one measure » rules (Kevin 2026-11-13).
- English. Plain words: subject, verb, object. One idea per sentence. Never mention our internal notes, our searches (« three of our searches found the story » fails), our signal rating ("weak buy") or earlier gaps, and no sentence the reader must read twice.
- Never a figure absent from the inputs. Never a price target, upside % or horizon Claude made up (the fair value is ours and computed: it may be quoted).
- No em or en dash, no hashtag, no link in an X post (the bio carries it).
- No contrast template (« X, not Y », « not X but Y »): state the one fact.
- Never write the disclaimer: « Not investment advice. » is added after the writer.
- Never a person's private data, never Thierry's name or handle on a sandbox account.
- The Sandbox accounts are marketing partners of Dualis Research, never Dualis itself (Thierry 2026-11-12): their bio says « Partnered with Dualis Research. ». A post built on a Dualis signal names its source in its last line, « Source: Dualis Research »; a Substack Note or Article links it to analysisparalysis888.substack.com, an X post carries no link (the bio does).

## The picture (every post carries one, Kevin 2026-11-11)
**The frame of every account, urgent or not (Kevin 2026-11-12):** when the post's company has drivers reversing NOW, the picture is drawn from the analysis, never from `image`: the share price against its fair value and the opportunity, then ONE block per reversing driver (1-3, strongest first): « NARRATIVE: A » (what the market believes, plain text) against « REALITY: not A » (what is happening, with its number), its strength (« STRONG INFLECTION ») and its chart with the two compared periods boxed and an arrow carrying the change. The TEXT tells the same story in the account's tone: the narrative, then the driver(s) turning, with the number on its chart; its first sentence leads with the first block's printed number, copied digit for digit (a text on the second block's margin under a first block of revenue +8.6% failed 2026-11-13, and so did two posts of one company sharing the same frame: the second takes another company or the `image` fallback) (« Q3 revenue +21% », never the price fall: SuperCom's Note opened on « down 34% » under a revenue block, and CareCloud's Note on 19.5% under a frame whose first block prints revenue +8.6%, both failed 2026-11-13; if the lead number is not the first block's, draw the single `image` chart of that number instead; a text saying 19.5% under a block that prints 19.6% failed 2026-11-13, and so did a text on a 34% price fall under a block « Q3 revenue +21% »: copy the first block's number digit for digit, and the block's chart must show that number, never a yearly bar chart where the boxed bar is +1%). A text on another measure (net debt, a macro exposure) takes the exposure's own single chart through `image`, never this frame. The frame is never reused for two posts of the day (two CareCloud posts shared one three-block frame on 2026-11-13), and never covers an Article, and block titles never say EPS or LTM. The `image` below is drawn only when no driver of the company reverses. HARD RULE, checked before the text is written: the frame is OFF for an Article, a second post of the same company, and any post whose first number is not printed digit for digit in the first block over a chart where that number is visible (SuperCom: block "+21%" over yearly bars at +1%; CareCloud: 19.5% under 19.6%; Lifeway Article with three blocks: all failed again 2026-11-13). Frame blocks never print a figure without its unit ("1.1M vs 6.3M" fails). When in doubt, use `image`: ONE chart, the note prints the text's lead number. The frame is allowed only for a signal post that is the only post of its company that day, whose first number equals the first block's printed number digit for digit; every other post of the day, every Article and every macro post uses `image` (2026-11-13 round 3: SuperCom, CareCloud x2 and Lifeway all still used the frame). Before writing, list the day's posts: at most ONE post per company uses the frame, and only if its text opens on the first block's printed number digit for digit; an Article never does (2026-11-13 round 3: SuperCom 34% under "+21%", CareCloud macro and CareCloud 19.5% under 19.6% on one shared frame, Lifeway Article with three blocks). DEFAULT TO `image`: in rounds 2 and 3 every post of the day, Notes and Articles alike, is drawn through `image` with ONE chart and no 'THE MARKET THINKS' block, even when drivers reverse (round 2 on 2026-11-13 still sent the frame under a SuperCom crash text, a CareCloud macro Note, a CareCloud 19.5% Note and a Lifeway Article); the three-block frame is OFF for every post of the day (a frame that failed is never redrawn; SuperCom, CareCloud x2 and Lifeway failed it again in round 2 on 2026-11-13): draw ONE `image` chart of the text's lead number. Choose the picture BEFORE the text: a text led by a price fall, a margin or a macro exposure never takes the frame (SuperCom, Obsidian, CareCloud and Lifeway all failed this on 2026-11-13); use the frame only when ALL are true: the text's first number equals the first block's printed number digit for digit (text 19.5% under block 19.6% or « Revenue up 8.6% » fails), the first block's chart shows that exact number (« Q3 revenue +21% » needs two quarterly bars, never yearly bars where the boxed bar is +1%). Otherwise draw the single `image` chart of the text's lead number (SuperCom, CareCloud and Obsidian failed this again 2026-11-13).
The writer writes that fallback picture with the text, in the same answer (`image`), and each tone skill's **Image** section decides its look. The code only draws. A reader who sees ONLY the picture must get, in five seconds: which company, what is measured, over which period, and the post's conclusion.
- **chart**: the listed chart of the SAME measure and period as the text's lead number, for an Article the one in its title (a title on Q3 profit before loan losses gets the quarterly chart of that measure, never the yearly profit chart, never `price` under a title about profit; one title carries one measure, not a price fall plus a profit drop); a margin text gets the single-line chart of that ONE margin (never a two-line chart such as gross and operating margin: the reader cannot decode the colours), a profit text the profit chart; a two-line chart is never used for a margin text, even with its series named in chartLabel ("White gross, orange operating" and "Gross margin white, operating orange" both failed 2026-11-12): if the only chart listed for the margin holds two lines, lead the text with another number that has a single-line chart (profit, sales); if the margin has only a two-line chart listed, lead the text with a number that has a single-line chart; `price` only when the claim is about the share price itself, never as a stand-in for a rate, margin, debt or profit claim. If no chart is listed for the lead number (net debt, one-off gain), lead the text with a number that has a chart.
- **point**: the one value that proves it; the arrow hits it. The chart shows distinct consecutive quarters or years: if two bars repeat the same value or a label is not a quarter end (Apr-26, Jul-26), take another listed chart or lead with a number whose chart is clean (RCM failed 2026-11-13).
- **note** (on the arrow): the proof with its number, which IS the point's value as listed and the number the text leads with; always a figure with its unit ("Sales $44.0M, down 4.3% in a year": the period is the comparison, never just "in Q3"): a % change names its period ("Close $15.80, down 13% since Oct 30"); ONE measure, the chart's own, in the axis unit and rounding ("2.9B yen", not "2,947M yen"); 3 to 5 words, so the box stays narrow and covers neither line, bars nor the last value label. A text about a one-off or a mix must show it on the picture (title or note), or lead with the number the picture proves.
- **chartLabel**: what is measured, its unit and period, in plain words (« Profit per quarter, $ millions »); printed as « Company: chartLabel ». Keep it under 40 characters: only measure and unit, never the period words of a price chart ("last 12 months": the picture adds them, and a repeat gets cut with "..."), no fair value, no % above fair value. The note sits in the empty half of the chart, never over the line or its last value (last point high: note low; last point low: note high; a line crossing the whole chart: note in the corner away from it), and the arrow with the end value is always visible. A label that prints "..." was too long: shorten it.
- **priceSays**: what the share price still assumes, the belief the point contradicts; printed as « VS price narrative: "…" ».
- **title**: the post's conclusion in 4 to 9 words, with its period (« Solera fell 21% in a week »). Printed big ONLY in the `breaking` style without a news card; otherwise the note carries the conclusion.
- **newsLine** + **news**: the news fact in plain words WITH the figure of the chart's measure (« CareCloud operating margin hits 19.5%, its best quarter ever »), `breaking` style, when a news item is listed, its most prestigious publisher. A superlative or a gain with no figure fails.
- **One story**: the picture says the SAME conclusion as the text, with the same main number: the figure in the text's first sentence is printed on the picture (title, note or news line). No number on the picture that the text contradicts, none that the inputs lack.
- **Plain words** a retail investor reads at once, in the text AND on the picture (news card and chart titles included: "Consensus EPS" and "Analyst mean EPS" failed 2026-11-13, write "Profit per share"): no EPS, adjusted EBITDA, EV/EBITDA, DCF, forward P/E, micro-cap, micro-company, net income, pre-provision income, price to book, book value, guide, cash floor, net cash floor, common shareholders, YoY, 8-K or other filing form names, 'below estimate' or '10% below what analysts expected' without the estimate's own figure, also printed on the picture; say profit, profit before loan losses, profit per share, shareholders, a year earlier, the company's forecast. Every claim carries its figure ("most profitable quarter ever" needs the profit in $).
- An Article's picture is its cover, always drawn through `image` as ONE chart (never the three-block frame, even when drivers reverse: no blocks, no « THE MARKET THINKS » text; Lifeway's Article carried three blocks a third time on 2026-11-13, the cover is the single-line margin chart whose note prints the title's 7.6%, and the title holds that one measure only, never the margin plus operating income plus the price gap), its single strongest proof with one note carrying the number in its title, and its title and subtitle carry the same one measure (Lifeway's cover held three charts and « 1.1M vs 6.3M » against the text's $3.8M, and failed again 2026-11-13); an Article text never says EBITDA, say operating profit.
- Each post has its own picture: never repeat the chart, point and words of another post of the day.

## The Sandbox (Business > Sandbox in the console, never published)
6 fake accounts: one X account and one Substack per tone (urgent Siren Small Caps, balanced Narrative Gap, neutral Ledger and Lens). Code: `web/src/collectors/dimensions/sandbox-posts/` reads THESE files at run time; a skill edit changes the next day's posts, never a past day (the skills' hash is stored on each post). Skill `image-correction` looks at each drawn picture and corrects its spec until clean (3 passes); skill `content-review` then reads each day's posts with their pictures; a failing post makes it edit these skills and the day is written again (3 rounds max).

### Daily mix per account (5 posts)
Every slot (08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00 Astana) is a signal post on the next of the day's best Ideas, one Idea per slot (fewer Ideas = fewer posts).

4 short posts (X post or Note) + 1 Article, the Article on the day's best Idea. Every account of a channel writes the same inputs in the same formats: only the tone differs, so Thierry compares tones with everything else equal.
