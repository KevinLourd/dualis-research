# Angle: signal

Our own edge: a narrative cracking or inflecting, read by our analysis. It is the angle that grows paid readers, because nobody else has it.

## Where the input comes from
- `gold.content_ideas` (the day's Ideas, scored per format; content-ideas), each citing its sources: Signal (`gold.signals`), Signal Changes (`gold.signal_changes`), Evidence (`gold.evidence`), Narrative Drivers (`gold.narrative_drivers`).
- The writer gets, per Idea: the title, why now, the narrative, the signal, the fair value vs quote, the cited evidence and drivers (`content-drafts/format.ts` `draftPrompt`).
- Sandbox: slots 08:00 and 14:00 take the day's best and second Ideas.

## How to write it
**Every signal post, X and Substack, every tone, walks these 5 steps in order (Kevin 2026-11-13: a post that skips one leaves the reader lost):**
1. **Signal type**: inflection (a gloomy story turning, price under fair value: a missed opportunity) or crack (a loved story breaking, price above: a danger); the type follows the step-2 gap: a price UNDER fair value is always an inflection (📈), never a crack (RCM 2026-11-16: "crack" over 5% under), with its strength (« Strong inflection »).
2. **Size**: quote vs fair value, both figures and the gap (« $3.10 vs $4.60 fair value: 33% under »).
3. **Trigger and date**: the fact that set it off, how recent, and its figure from the inputs (« Q3 results, Nov 10: sales +21% to $7.5M »); for a BUY the trigger is a fact that turned UP, never a fall or a loss, and never a close or a 12-month low alone (Monro "shares hit $10.36, a 12-month low" and RCM "shares closed at $31.54 after the Nov 12 report" failed 2026-11-16 round 6): with no upturn in the inputs, the trigger line is the dated close vs fair value ("Nov 13: close $31.54, fair value $33.18"), never a fall or "shares fell X%" (RCM, Monro 2026-11-16 round 5), and the belief line also holds a dated figure ("Nov 13: shares down 12% since Oct 30, now $31.54") (RCM "revenue $73.4M vs $93.8M" and Monro "sales down 1.8%, net loss" 2026-11-16 failed): if the inputs hold no upturn (RCM 2026-11-16 round 2 again printed « revenue $73.4M vs $93.8M in Q2 »: a fall, never a BUY trigger), the trigger line is the dated price move, with one figure (« Nov 12: shares fell to $31.54 »), and the word "loss" replaces "net loss"; a trigger with no figure (« Q3 report, Nov 12. », « Q3 results published » (RCM 2026-11-16 round 4), « guidance raised », « the close came the day after the report », RCM 2026-11-16) fails, and so does a figure with an unexplained noun (« the rail exit freed $62M of security »): say what the money is (« released cash $62M »); the word « security » never follows a $ figure (Enviri 2026-11-16 again); a close printed in the trigger line is the SAME close as step 2, digit for digit (Monro 2026-11-16 round 4: $10.36 in the trigger under $10.48 in step 2 and on the picture), and the belief line always carries one dated figure (« Price assumes no earnings recovery » fails: no figure); the trigger line and the belief line print the SAME measure (CareCloud 2026-11-15: trigger operating income $6.6M beside a belief on a 19.5% margin fails; Musashi: a 2,688M yen one-off beside operating income fails), and every « A vs B » figure names its period.
4. **Rationale, point by point** (the trigger of step 3 and the belief/number here are ONE measure, the one the text's picture note charts; a one-off gain beside a profit-per-share belief fails, Musashi 2026-11-15): the price trades under (or above) fair value because the market believes <the drivers, a few words each>; then one line per driver that turned: « Nov 10: <driver> turned, <its number> », never the same fact as the trigger line of step 3 (Enviri 2026-11-15 repeated "rail exit frees $62M" in both) and never a different measure from the belief it follows. The belief, the trigger and the number are the same measure (a trigger on a 2,688M yen one-off beside a belief on operating income is two measures, as in Musashi 2026-11-15 round 4: trigger on the measure the picture charts) (every belief line carries one figure with its unit and period, never "Market believes Monro can no longer earn money" or "cannot turn work into cash" (Monro, Enviri 2026-11-16 round 5), and never "as if the recovery is over" or "as if sales keep shrinking" (RCM, Monro 2026-11-16 round 6: no figure): the belief line prints a dated figure from the inputs ("sales fell 1.8% in Q2"), or the price's own dated move ("down 34% since Oct 30") when the inputs hold no result figure; a trigger line is a dated fact with its figure, never "shares closed at $10.48" alone; belief 'losses last through 2027' pairs with a forecast figure; belief 'share sales weigh' never pairs with an operating margin), and every money figure carries its currency ($ or yen) and period (« profit per share 130 to 307 yen », never « 130 to 307 »).
5. **Conclusion** (its LAST characters are the gap's number and %, e.g. « Still under fair value by 5% »; « Shares still sit 5% under fair value » ends in a word and fails RCM 2026-11-16 round 4; it ends with a digit: « The price still has X wrong » with no number failed CareCloud, RCM, Enviri and Monro 2026-11-16 round 4): one sentence, what the price still has wrong, carrying the step-2 gap or the step-3 figure ("Shares still sit 36% under fair value"); never a belief or conclusion with no number ("the market doubts the recovery will last", RCM, Monro, CareCloud 2026-11-16).
