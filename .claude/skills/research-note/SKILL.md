# Research note

## Thierry's frame (call 2026-11-04, `docs/thierry-method.md`)
Price = the market's **narrative**; **fundamental value** = what the business is worth if the plan works (comparables, normalized earnings, e.g. 10x EV/EBITDA → share price). Long = price below fundamental, the bad narrative inflects; short = price above, the hyped narrative cracks. Every note says: price vs fundamental (above/below), the narrative behind the gap, what would crack or inflect it. Claude never invents a price target, upside % or horizon: those are Thierry's numbers; Claude gives the narrative, the evidence and KPIs vs analyst consensus.

## Thesis card (watchlist, one per ticker)
- **Ticker / company / sector / market cap** (as of date)
- **Thesis** in ONE sentence: the narrative the market prices, and why it is cracking / inflecting.
- **Map** (his Obsidian graph): product, business model (margins, moat, switching costs, market structure), revenue split, industry, risks, leading indicators.
- **Drivers** (3-5): each = a measurable variable + direction + why it moves the stock; prefer KPIs with a consensus estimate and leading indicators correlated with sales.
- **Catalysts**: dated events (earnings, product, regulation) that prove or kill the thesis.
- **Kill criteria**: what would make us wrong. Written up front.
- **Signals to watch**: news keywords + data series the daily watch tracks per driver.
- Source of each claim: link. Inputs first: core filings (5 years), his Excel model, his notes. Prefilled text shows as plain editable text (never labelled Claude's); Thierry edits and owns it.

## Article anatomy (Substack + website, same order)
1. Title: the claim with its figures, not the topic (Multibagger Ideas: « 60%+ Revenue Growth, an Order Book Bigger Than the Company, Under 10x Earnings »). A closed call gets its own post titled with its result (« Thesis Closed at +81% in 15 Months »).
2. Key takeaways: 3 bullets above the fold (free).
3. The setup: what the market believes now.
4. Our view + drivers, with 1-2 charts.
5. Catalysts and timeline.
6. Risks / kill criteria.
7. Valuation: scenarios (bear / base / bull) with the math shown.
8. Paywall sits after section 3 or 4.
9. Disclaimer (always): "Not investment advice. For information only. Author may hold positions in securities discussed."

## Rules
- Numbers carry date + source. No figure without a source.
- English only. Short sentences, one idea each. No em dash.
- Thierry's voice wins over Claude's: Claude drafts, Thierry decides.
