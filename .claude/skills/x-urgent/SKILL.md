# X, urgent tone

The « BREAKING » style of the biggest finance accounts: a headline, a cashtag, a chart (the Kobeissi Letter pattern, observed: https://superx.so/creators/KobeissiLetter). It wins attention; studies show clickbait headlines cost credibility, most for small, unknown sources (https://mospace.umsystem.edu/xmlui/handle/10355/56080). It is in the Sandbox so Thierry can judge that trade-off on our own Ideas. Every house rule stays (skill `content-matrix`): the number must be in the inputs, the claim must be true.

## X post
- **Signal post (BUY or SELL)**: the 5 lines of skill `angle-signal` instead, spaced, at most 250 characters on X, 600 on a Note; the one-line « 🚨 BREAKING ... Wake up. » form is never used for an Idea (RCM and Proficient, 2026-11-14 rounds 1 and 2); an Idea with no BUY or SELL gets the 2 lines of `angle-signal` (close vs fair value with its gap; one reason with figure and period), no 🚨, no second figure (RCM's 11.7% fall and Proficient's $10.54 on Feb 3 failed).
- **Length**: at most 200 characters.
- **Hook**: opens `🚨 BREAKING: <short name>` then ONE shock number in the first 60 characters (a BUY or SELL signal post never uses this one-line form: it walks the 5 lines of `angle-signal` and ends with « Source: Dualis Research »).
- **Structure**: the shock number, then one line of urgency (« Nobody is pricing this. », « The market has not caught up. »); if that line says the price is high or low, it carries the fair value gap with its number (« $7220.T trades 13% above our fair value. »). `$TICKER` once. ONE measure only: no second figure or claim (gross margin, net cash, fair value gap) that the picture does not show, and no claim without its number. The shock number is copied digit for digit from the picture's first block or note (block prints 19.6%: write 19.6%; block prints 'Q3 revenue +21%': never open on the price fall). A macro post names the exposure with its own figure and unit in the text (e.g. 'net debt $38.2M'), never 'oil moves its cash flow' alone.
- **Allowed**: one word in CAPS, one exclamation mark, « just », « wake up », « nobody is talking about this ».
- **Visual**: see Image below.
- Example:
```
🚨 BREAKING: CareCloud just QUADRUPLED profit for shareholders to $4.7M. $CCLD still trades like nothing happened. Nobody is pricing this.
```
- Do: lead with the biggest move in the inputs (a multiple, a % change).
- Don't: invent stakes (« going to zero », « 10x incoming »), promise a gain, more than one emoji, a hashtag. A promissory claim is the line regulators draw (FINRA Rule 2210 standard: https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Regulatory-Notice-26-14.pdf).

## X Article
- **Length**: 400 to 700 words, short punchy paragraphs (1-2 sentences).
- **Title**: `BREAKING:` + the shock number, at most 90 characters; the shock number is the signal's first driver number (« revenue +21% to $7.5M »), never a price fall the picture does not chart (SuperCom Article, 2026-11-13: crash title over a debt and revenue frame) (« BREAKING: CareCloud Quadrupled Shareholder Profit and Wall Street Missed It »).
- **Subtitle**: one line of urgency.
- **Structure** (`##` headings): `## The number`, `## Why nobody saw it`, `## What happens next`, `## The risk`.
- **Visual**: see Image below.
- Do: one bold line per section with its figure.
- Don't: drop the risk section; fake scarcity (« last chance »); jargon such as 'dilute', '200-day average', 'multiple of operating profit' (say: more shares sold, average price of the last 200 days, a multiple of operating profit explained in plain words or left out); a subtitle on another measure than the title; any line on the signal's own strength (« Our signal is weak, not strong » failed SuperCom 2026-11-14).

## Image
- **style**: `breaking` (news card + price card).
- **title**: the alarm with its shock number, one word in CAPS allowed (« Profit QUADRUPLED in a year »).
- **newsLine**: when a news item is listed, the fact that triggered the post, with its number.
- **note**: the shock number itself, on the point that shows it; the box sits in the empty half of the chart (last point high: box low, last point low: box high) and never covers the line or a bar.
- **chart**: the series where the move is biggest, of the SAME measure as the shock number (a revenue text gets the quarterly revenue chart, never `price`). The note, priceSays and picture never contradict the text: a text saying 'the story broke' never sits over a note saying 'still intact' (RCM, 2026-11-13). A macro post ends on its exposure figure, never a vague closer ('Risk mood matters').
