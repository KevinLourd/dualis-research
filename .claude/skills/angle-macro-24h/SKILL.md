# Angle: macro-24h (newsjacking)

React while the story breaks: add our angle in the first hours, the window closes around 24 h (David Meerman Scott's newsjacking, https://openviewpartners.com/blog/newsjacking-dos-and-donts/, https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/newsjacking/). Scheduled events (CPI, central bank meetings, earnings) are known ahead: be ready for them (https://www.contentgrip.com/newsjacking/).

## Where the input comes from
- `gold.macro_news` (dimension `macro-news`): Google News RSS searches on markets, rates, inflation, central banks, oil, tariffs, small caps, Japan, Korea; one item per article with every query that found it, last 24 h. Free, no key.
- Our tickers' own headlines of the last 24 h (`gold.news_items`, dimension `news`) and their narratives, so the post ties the macro story to a name we cover.
- Sandbox: slot 11:00; the input = the headline found by the most searches, newest first.

## How to write it
- Open on the headline's fact (with its source when it is a big name: « per Reuters »).
- Then the tie: which covered company it moves and how (rates and a leveraged balance sheet, oil and a shipper's costs, yen and an exporter), with that exposure's listed figure and unit in the text (a tie with no number fails: "Obsidian still prices a producer that burns cash" had none, and "Obsidian follows crude" over a "Consensus EPS" picture failed again 2026-11-13: the picture is the exposure's own chart, never a profit per share chart) and the same figure on the picture.
- Always tie it to ONE covered company, the most exposed one, and write that exposure's listed figure with its unit in the Note itself (« Obsidian net debt $X »): a macro Note with no number, or sitting on a profit-per-share chart, fails (Obsidian failed this again 2026-11-13), with the exposure's own figure and unit (debt, interest cost, cash) written in the text and drawn as its single chart through `image`, never a profit per share chart (the Obsidian Note had no figure and a « Consensus EPS » picture, failed 2026-11-13) (the writer names its ticker), never one already used by another post of the day, with the exposure's figure in the text (the Obsidian oil post had no number and sat on the EPS frame, 2026-11-13), and its picture is that exposure's single chart, never the three-block inflection frame (a CareCloud macro Note under the revenue, margin and « Analyst mean EPS » frame failed 2026-11-13), and draw the exposure's own listed chart through `image`, never the three-block revenue, margin and profit frame (the CareCloud Nvidia post carried that frame, 2026-11-13), and state the exposure with a listed number (debt, interest cost, share of sales), which is the text's lead figure and the picture's only chart through `image`, never the three-block driver frame (a macro Note on that frame, with « small companies that carry debt » and no debt figure, failed 2026-11-13); FIRST check that this exposure has a listed chart (none = another company), because title, text and cover all sit on that chart and a price chart never covers a macro Article; the chart is the listed chart of that exposure itself (debt, interest cost, cash), never the share price (a post whose picture is the price chart fails, and so does a title or picture heading that pairs the headline's yield with a share price fall or a fair value gap (« 10-Year Yield Tops 5%. CareCloud Trades 32% Under Fair Value » over a price chart failed 2026-11-12): it carries the one exposure number, e.g. « 10-Year Yield Tops 5%. CareCloud Net Debt Hit $38.2M »), an Article cover included: the exposure number in its title (net debt) gets that exposure's chart, and if none is listed, pick another company, the text leads with the exposure number ($38.2M net debt) and not the macro figure, the note prints that one number with its unit ("Debt $38.2M, up from $1.2M"), and the text leads with that same number; priceSays is the belief about that exposure ("Debt costs will stay low"), never a profit claim. HARD STOP: a macro Note whose text has no figure with its unit, or whose picture is a frame or a profit per share chart, is rewritten before answering (Obsidian again, 2026-11-13); if the exposure number has no listed chart, or the only picture you could draw is `price`, do not write a macro Article on that company: pick another covered company whose exposure (debt, interest cost, cash) has a listed chart BEFORE writing the title, give the exposure a figure with its unit in the text ("net debt $38.2M", never "debt has risen"), and never write "searches" or "profit per share" in the Article; a macro Article's cover is NEVER the price chart, so its title never says 'Trades X% Under Fair Value' and its text never mentions our searches; and before answering read the cover's chartLabel: if it says "Share price", the Article is not done, change company and rewrite title, text and note on the exposure chart; if the cover's chartLabel would read "Share price", the title, the text's lead number and priceSays cannot be about debt or a rate (a net debt title over a "Share price" cover failed 2026-11-12, again in round 2). A title or text naming debt over a price chart fails, and so does an Article title about a share price jump (« CareCloud Shares Jumped 51% ») over a yield and debt text: the title carries the exposure number and the cover is that exposure's chart, and an Article keeps ONE measure (the exposure and the rate), never margin, share count or forecasts too.

## Check before answering
- Before writing, pick the company whose exposure (net debt, interest cost) has a listed chart; the Note's text carries that figure with its unit ("Obsidian net debt $X") and the picture is that chart, never an EPS frame (Obsidian oil Note failed 2026-11-13 round 3).
- The picture is `image` with the exposure's own single chart (net debt, interest cost), the text's lead figure is printed on it, and it is never the three-block frame (CareCloud failed 2026-11-13). The tie names the headline's own driver (a rate headline ties to debt cost; an oil headline to an oil-cost company), never two drivers in one sentence.

## Never
- Invent the tie: name a company only when the narrative or the inputs support the link.
- Newsjack a tragedy (war deaths, disasters).
- A figure the headline or the inputs do not give.
